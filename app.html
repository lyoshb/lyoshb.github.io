01Voice

The keyboard is optional.

Hold the button and say it the way you'd say it to a person — "remind me to send the invoice tomorrow at three". It becomes a reminder with a real clock time, filed under Work. It rings when it matters.

Understands run-on sentences, two errands in one breath, and "in a couple of hours".

03Order

A tidy mind, color-coded.

Every task lands in a category — five built in, the rest are yours to name and color. Voice files things automatically; when nothing fits, it politely invents a new box.

These are draggable. In the app they keep themselves in a grid; here they're on a day off.

aLock Screen countdown
