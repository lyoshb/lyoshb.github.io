Built around one habit: just say it

🎙️

Voice in, plan out

Hold, speak, done. AI understands mixed phrases, splits several tasks in one breath and resolves "in a couple hours" into real times.

⚡️

Works everywhere

Dual AI routing — one engine worldwide, another inside mainland China. If both are unreachable, an on-device parser takes over. Never a dead button.

⏳

Live Activity countdown

The next reminder lives on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island with a live countdown — no need to open the app.

🧩

Widgets that match

Home-screen plan, progress ring, month at a glance, one-tap voice add from the Lock Screen.

🌍

5 languages, instantly

English, 中文, Русский, Español, Deutsch — switch in-app and everything follows at once, widgets included. Voice understands each.

🔒

Private by design

Tasks never leave the device. No analytics, no ads, no account database. Delete the app — the data is gone.
