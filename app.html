Say it.
It's planned.

A day planner built around one habit: you talk, it organizes. Tasks, reminders, categories and time — from a single spoken sentence.

The same morning, in ink and in paper.

01Voice

The keyboard is optional.

Hold the button and say it the way you'd say it to a person — "remind me to send the invoice tomorrow at three". It becomes a reminder with a real clock time, filed under Work. It rings when it matters.

Understands run-on sentences, two errands in one breath, and "in a couple of hours".

02For every age
Try it — tap a circle 0 of 3

Day complete — the streak lives on.

If you can talk, you can plan.

No manuals, no settings maze. One button, big type, colors that mean something. The first minute feels familiar at eight or at eighty — checking things off feels the same as crossing them out on paper.

Go on — the circles on the left are real.

03Order

A tidy mind, color-coded.

Every task lands in a category — five built in, the rest are yours to name and color. Voice files things automatically; when nothing fits, it politely invents a new box.

These are draggable. In the app they keep themselves in a grid; here they're on a day off.

04The fine print, proudly

Small things, done properly.

aLock Screen countdown

The next reminder lives on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with a live timer. The phone stays in your pocket.

bWidgets that match

Today's plan, a progress ring, the month at a glance — and one-tap voice capture right from the Lock Screen.

cFive languages, instantly

English, 中文, Русский, Español, Deutsch. Switch inside the app and everything follows at once — widgets included.

dWorks in mainland China

Two AI engines routed by reachability, an on-device parser underneath. The voice button never plays dead.

ePrivate by default

Tasks never leave the device. No analytics, no ads, no account database. Delete the app — the data is gone.

fQuiet by design

Monochrome ink, one reserved accent, a notification chime you won't flinch at. Nothing shouts.
